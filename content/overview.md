# landal.com

<p class="stats-line">82 captured pages, distilled into 39 page types and 35 reusable components.</p>

<!-- stat-blocks -->

<p class="callout"><strong>Light pass.</strong> This covers landal.com (the /en site). Every captured page is placed and linked with its own screenshot, and 35 components are identified and linked with a cropped example image and the pages that use them. None of it has a written description or a CMS data model yet. The sitemap lists 5,708 pages. The pages shown here are a sample of those, picked by the page types the site declares in its own markup: 2 to 5 pages per type, plus pages that show a kind of section nothing else does and types that only appear in the sitemap.</p>

## How this was made

<ol class="how-steps">
  <li>
    <div>
      <p class="step-title">Survey and capture the site</p>
      <p class="step-body">Follow the menu, footer and links on pages, read the sitemap, and group the pages that are built alike. Pick a sample from each group and take a full-page screenshot of each.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li>
    <div>
      <p class="step-title">Analyze the structure</p>
      <p class="step-body">Break each captured page down into its underlying structure, find the sections it is built from, and spot the ones that are reused across different pages.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li>
    <div>
      <p class="step-title">Document what's there</p>
      <p class="step-body">Place every page type and component and link it to its screenshot or cropped example. For this pass there are no written descriptions or CMS write-ups, and similar components are grouped with their variants.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li>
    <div>
      <p class="step-title">Publish it</p>
      <p class="step-body">Assemble everything into this browsable site.</p>
    </div>
  </li>
</ol>

Component inventory

landal.com

82 captured pages, distilled into 39 page types and 35 reusable components.

35Components82Pages

Light pass. This covers landal.com (the /en site). Every captured page is placed and linked with its own screenshot, and 35 components are identified and linked with a cropped example image and the pages that use them. None of it has a written description or a CMS data model yet. The sitemap lists 5,708 pages. The pages shown here are a sample of those, picked by the page types the site declares in its own markup: 2 to 5 pages per type, plus pages that show a kind of section nothing else does and types that only appear in the sitemap.

How this was made

  1. Survey and capture the site

    Follow the menu, footer and links on pages, read the sitemap, and group the pages that are built alike. Pick a sample from each group and take a full-page screenshot of each.

  2. Analyze the structure

    Break each captured page down into its underlying structure, find the sections it is built from, and spot the ones that are reused across different pages.

  3. Document what's there

    Place every page type and component and link it to its screenshot or cropped example. For this pass there are no written descriptions or CMS write-ups, and similar components are grouped with their variants.

  4. Publish it

    Assemble everything into this browsable site.
